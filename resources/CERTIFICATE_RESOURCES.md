# Volterra: Certificate & Completion Guidelines

To claim your participation credentials or project completion badge for this workshop, please follow the steps outlined below.

---

## 📋 Steps to Claim Certification

1.  **Replicate the Model**: Clone the repository or open the Colab notebook and run the entire data cleaning, training, and diagnostics flow.
2.  **Add a Custom Feature**: Modify the code to add a custom feature to the training pipeline (e.g. adding a scaling transformation or introducing another input attribute).
3.  **Deploy Your App**: Deploy your customized Streamlit dashboard live on Hugging Face Spaces or Streamlit Community Cloud.
4.  **Submit Your Project**: Send the URL of your public GitHub repository and your live deployment link to the review console/mentor email.

Once your submission is reviewed, a digital **Certificate of Completion** will be dispatched to your registered email address.
